Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how software and Chrome extensions developed by Jesus De Lira (including Semantic Shield and Dual-Memory Optimizer) handle user privacy and data.

1. Sovereign & Local-First Principle

We believe user data belongs to the user. Our extensions are architected from the ground up as sovereign, local-first tools. All computations, syntax parsing, prompt condensation, secret scrubbing, and network filtering run strictly on your local device.

2. Data Collection and Transmission

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect, log, sell, or share names, email addresses, browsing history, keystrokes, or IP addresses.
  • No External Telemetry: Our extensions do not contain third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or tracking pixels).
  • No Remote Code: Our extensions do not load or execute external remote scripts, complying strictly with Chrome Web Store Manifest V3 guidelines.

3. Storage and Permissions

  • Local Storage: When an extension stores settings, custom rules, or prompt directives, they are saved strictly in Chrome's local storage sandbox (chrome.storage.local or chrome.storage.sync) on your machine.
  • Declarative Net Request: Network protection rules (such as blocking cross-origin requests to local LLM ports like 127.0.0.1:11434) are evaluated natively inside Chromium's network engine without intercepting packet contents.

4. Third-Party Web Services

Our extensions only interact with websites or web AI chats that you explicitly navigate to. We do not intermediate your connection or send your credentials or prompt content to any server operated by us.
